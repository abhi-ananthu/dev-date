## Introduction

Welcome to Topsdraw! We value your privacy and are committed to providing a transparent and secure experience on our platform.This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect from users and visitors of www.topsdraw.com, and how we handle it. By subscribing to our services or otherwise agreeing to this Privacy Policy, you consent to the collection, use, disclosure, and other handling of your information as described below. If you have any concerns about providing information to us or having it displayed on our website or otherwise used as per this Privacy Policy, please refrain from using our services.

## The Information We Collect

**1. User-Submitted Information**  
We collect personal details such as your name, email address, and any other information you share with us through forms, emails, or communications.

**2. Verified Information**  
To maintain service quality, we confirm and publish key details you provide (e.g., name, location, industry) when building your provider profile or business listing.

**3. Information Shared with Others**  
Information you publish (reviews, profiles) is visible to other users. Do not share sensitive personal data in public sections.

**4. Client-related Data**  
We may collect client feedback and service reviews from individuals who have interacted with providers. This feedback helps improve user experience.

**5. Anonymous & Aggregated Information**  
We use data analytics tools to collect anonymized usage patterns. This helps us improve the site experience, troubleshoot issues, and enhance content.

**6. Browser & Technical Info**  
We collect technical data like browser type, device used, IP address, and referral URLs to help us maintain and optimize performance.

## Use Of Information

We use collected information to:  
- Improve our services  
- Communicate updates  
- Monitor misuse  
- Ensure compliance with policies  

## Data Security and Privacy Measures

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to safeguard your personal data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

## Access To Information, Correction, Or Deletion

You can request to review, update, or delete your personal information at any time by contacting us at privacy@topsdraw.com.

## Disclosure Of Information

We may share your data:  
- With service providers under confidentiality agreements

## Data Breach Notification Procedures

In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected users via email or site notice in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

Your information may be transferred to and maintained on servers located outside your country of residence. By using our services, you consent to this transfer.

## Privacy Of Minors

Topsdraw does not knowingly collect data from individuals under the age of 18. If we become aware, we will promptly delete such data.
